Heart Voice Quality Test

Which one sounds best?

The same Heart sample in three sizes. Turn your iPad's ringer switch or silent mode off and the volume up. Listen through the speaker from a few steps away.

  1. A. Current: MP3 at 48k

    Full quality. About 96 MB for all four decades.

  2. B. Recommended: AAC at 32k

    About a third smaller (about 66 MB). Measured: no loss.

  3. C. Smallest: MP3 at 24k

    Half the size (about 48 MB). Measured: duller s and t sounds.

Tap a Play button. Tap it again to stop.

If B sounds the same as A to you, B is the one to use: same quality, a much smaller download.
